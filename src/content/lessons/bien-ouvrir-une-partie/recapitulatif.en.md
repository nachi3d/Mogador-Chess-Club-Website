Four rules, in order:

1. **Take the centre** with a pawn.
2. **Get your pieces out**, knights first, once each.
3. **Castle early.**
4. **Keep your queen home** until she has a safe square.

If you do not know what to play in the opening, just ask yourself: *which of my pieces is doing the least right now?* Then move it. That is almost always the right move.
