Your king starts the game in the middle of the board. That is the worst place for him: it is exactly where lines open when pawns get traded.

**Castling** fixes it in one move. The king goes to a corner behind three untouched pawns, and on the way your rook comes towards the centre. Two problems solved at once: the king is safe, the rook is in play.

**Castle early.** Not "once I have finished developing", not "when it gets dangerous". Early. Most beginner games are lost by a king left in the centre while the position opened around him.

**Two rules to know:**

- You cannot castle if your king or that rook has already moved — even if it came back.
- You cannot castle out of check, or through an attacked square. The rook, however, may cross an attacked square: it is the king that counts.
