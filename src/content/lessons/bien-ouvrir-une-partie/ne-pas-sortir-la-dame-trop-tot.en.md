The queen is your strongest piece, and that is exactly why you should not bring her out first.

A queen in the middle of the board on move three is a giant target. Every time your opponent attacks her with a minor piece, you have to move her — and while you move the same piece four times, they develop four different ones. You do not lose material: you lose the game more slowly, behind on everything.

<!--board-->

**When can she come out?** When she has a safe square where the opponent cannot harass her, usually once the minor pieces are developed. The queen is a middlegame piece, not an opening one.

To see what neglected development and a king left in the centre really cost, go and look at [Légal's mate](/en/pieges/legal/).
